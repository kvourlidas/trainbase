Your body adapts to whatever you ask of it. To keep getting stronger, ask for a little more each time.

## Three ways to progress

- **More weight** for the same reps.
- **More reps** with the same weight.
- **More sets** across the week.

Change one thing at a time and log every set, so you always know where to start next session.
