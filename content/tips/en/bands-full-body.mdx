With one set of resistance bands you can train your whole body in under 40 minutes.

## Sample workout

- Banded squat — 3×12
- Banded row — 3×12
- Banded chest press — 3×10
- Banded Romanian deadlift — 3×12
- Pallof press — 3×10 per side

When the reps get easy, move up to a heavier band.
