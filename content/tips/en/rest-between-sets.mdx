Rest between sets is part of the program, not wasted time.

## A simple guide

- **Heavy compound lifts** (squat, deadlift): 2–3 minutes.
- **Accessory exercises**: 60–90 seconds.
- **Small muscle groups**: 45–60 seconds.

If your technique breaks down on the next set, rest a little longer.
